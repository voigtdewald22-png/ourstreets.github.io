Our Streets,
Our Words

A digital language map built with a Grade 4 class in mind — every stop is a real place in their own neighbourhood, built around three languages learners already live in: Sesotho, Afrikaans and English.

The wall we're breaking:English treated as the only language that counts in class — cutting learners off from the Sesotho and Afrikaans they already think, joke and belong in.

No data or smartphone at home?

A printed A3 version of this map with the same six stops is handed out in class, with the matching cards printed as a cut-and-paste activity instead of a tap-to-match screen.

Shared devices

Pairs can work on one tablet in class time — one learner drives the screen, the other reads the language cards aloud, then they swap.

Your progress stays on your device

Nothing here is uploaded anywhere. Progress saves only in this browser, so a teacher would collect it by screenshot or by watching the live showcase.

Functional demonstration built for ENIP322 — Assignment 3: Classroom Without Walls.
